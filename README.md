# ShifoCare - Habka Maamulka Isbitaalka (Hospital Management System)

Nidaam dhameystiran oo casri ah oo loogu talagalay maamulka iyo kormeerka howlaha isbitaalka (Hospital Management System - HMS), kaasoo lagu dhisay **HTML5**, **CSS3**, iyo **Vanilla JavaScript**.

---

## 🌟 7-da Jadwal ee Ugu Muhiimsan Isbitaalka (7 Core Tables)

1. **Bukaannada (Patients Management Table)**
   - Diiwaangelinta bukaanka (ID, Magaca, Da'da, Jinsiga, Kooxda Dhiigga, Telefoonka, Qolka/Qeybta, Dhakhtarka, Xaaladda: Jiifa, Bukaan Socod, Fasaxan, Degdeg).

2. **Dhaqaatiirta (Doctors & Medical Staff Table)**
   - Xogta dhaqaatiirta takhasusyada kala duwan leh (Wadnaha, Qalliinka, Haweenka, Carruurta, Lafaha, Indhaha, Shift-ka shaqada, iyo bukaannada u qoran).

3. **Ballamaha (Appointments Schedule Table)**
   - Jadwalka ballamaha maalinlaha ah (Patient, Dhakhtar, Qeybta, Taariikhda/Waqtiga, Nooca Booqashada, Qiimaha/Fee, Xaaladda: La Sugayo, La Xaqiijiyay, Dhameystiran).

4. **Sariiraha & Qolalka (Beds & Ward Management Table)**
   - Maareynta qeybaha isbitaalka (ICU, Qeybta Guud, Umusha & Dhallaanka, VIP), dabaqa, nooca sariirta, bukaanka jiifa, kalkalisada mas'uulka ka ah, iyo qiimaha maalintii.

5. **Farmashiyaha & Daawooyinka (Pharmacy & Drug Inventory Table)**
   - Kaydka daawooyinka (Koodhka, Magaca daawada, Qeybta, Shirkadda sameysay, Tirada hadhay, Qiimaha, Taariikhda dhicitaanka, iyo heerka kaydka).

6. **Shaybaarka & Baaritaannada (Laboratory & Diagnostic Tests Table)**
   - Diwaangelinta tijaabooyinka (CBC, Raajada, Ultrasound, Troponin, Malaria), farsamo-yaqaanka, natiijada: Caadi, Khatar/Sare, ama La Sugayo.

7. **Biilasha & Maaliyadda (Invoicing & Revenue Table)**
   - Qaansheeyooyinka lacag-bixinta (Wadarta biilka, Lacagta la bixiyay, Haraaga deynta, Habka bixinta: EVC Plus, E-Dahab, Kaarka Bangiga, Kaash).

---

## ✨ Tilmaamaha & Faa'iidooyinka Gaarka ah (Key Features)

- **Interactive Dashboard & KPIs**: 6 xabo oo kaararka tirakoobka ah (Wadarta bukaannada, Dhaqaatiirta shaqada joogta, Ballamaha maanta, Sariiraha bannaan, Daawooyinka yar, Dakhliga guud).
- **Chart.js Visual Analytics**:
  - Garaafka imaatinka bukaan socodka iyo bukaan jiifka 7-dii maalmood ee la soo dhaafay.
  - Garaafka wareega (Doughnut chart) ee qaybsanaanta sariiraha ICU, Guud, Umusha, iyo VIP.
- **Full CRUD Capabilities**: Awood buuxda oo ah Ku dar (Add), Wax ka bedel (Edit), Tirtir (Delete), iyo Eeg (View) oo ku saleysan popup dynamic modal.
- **Real-Time Search & Filters**: Qeyb kasta waxaad ku dhex raadin kartaa magac, ID, ama xaalad gaar ah.
- **Export to CSV**: Dhoofinta xogta jadwal kasta qaab Excel/CSV ah adigoo hal badhan riixaya.
- **Print Reports**: Daabacaad diyaar u ah warbixinnada maamulka.
- **Dark Mode / Light Mode**: Isbedelka iftiinka shaashadda oo la keydinayo.
- **LocalStorage Data Persistence**: Xog kasta oo aad ku darto ama ka tirtirto waxay toos ugu keydsanaysaa browser-kaaga.
- **Responsive Design**: Wuxuu si heer sare ah ugu shaqeynayaa Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, iyo Desktop.

---

## 🚀 Sida Loo Furo / How to Run

1. Guji ama laba jeer dhufo faylka **`index.html`** si aad ugu furto browser-kaaga (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
2. Wax software ama server kale oo loo baahan yahay ma jiro – waa pure standard HTML, CSS, iyo JavaScript.
